2. Scope of this policy

This policy explains what personal information Mytrima collects, why, how it is used and shared, and what rights you have over it. It applies to: (a) Tenant staff who create and use Mytrima accounts, and (b) a Tenant's end customers whose data is processed through the Mytrima platform on that Tenant's behalf (for example, via WhatsApp conversations, growth diagnostics, surveys, or ratings).

Where Mytrima processes end-customer data on behalf of a Tenant business, that Tenant — not Mytrima — is generally the responsible party/data controller for that data under POPIA, and this policy should be read alongside that Tenant's own customer-facing notices. 4. How we use this information

  • To provide the core service: business diagnostics, customer messaging, ratings, surveys, and payment facilitation
  • To compute and display the Growth Audit score, NPS, and rating aggregates to the relevant Tenant
  • To detect and follow up on dissatisfied customers (NPS detractors) on a Tenant's behalf
  • To moderate ratings before they are published (pending → public/hidden)
  • To maintain security, prevent fraud/abuse, and meet legal and accounting obligations
  • To communicate with Tenant staff about their account and the service

We do not sell personal information.

5. Legal basis for processing

Consent is treated as a first-class, per-category record — not a single blanket flag (Master Plan Section 11). Depending on the category of data and how it was collected, the lawful basis recorded is one of:

  • Consent — e.g. WhatsApp marketing messages, review publication
  • Contract — e.g. data needed to deliver a service a Tenant or customer requested
  • Legitimate interest — e.g. basic security and fraud-prevention logging
  • Legal obligation — e.g. retaining certain records for accounting or regulatory purposes

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we (or a Tenant, via us) hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, or export of it (Data Subject Access Request / DSAR)
  • Withdraw consent for a specific data category at any time, without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za, or the equivalent Lesotho authority once confirmed

11. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, role-based access control, tenant data isolation enforced at the database layer, and immutable audit logging of access and changes (Master Plan Section 10). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
